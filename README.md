## 7. PHARMACEUTICAL PRODUCT SAFETY

Because this is a pharmaceutical demo, implement responsible product presentation.

- Clearly distinguish prescription-only medicines from non-prescription healthcare products.
- Do not offer unrestricted direct checkout for prescription-only medicines.
- Where a product requires a prescription or professional verification, show “Prescription / Verification Required” and a Request Information action.
- Do not invent drug indications, dosage instructions, contraindications, or guaranteed therapeutic benefits.
- Do not include controlled medicines as ordinary retail products.
- Display demo prices only as illustrative values.
- Include a notice that product availability, pricing, regulatory status, and prescription requirements must be verified before launch.
- Do not provide medical diagnosis or personalized treatment recommendations.
- Do not make unsupported claims about curing or preventing diseases.
